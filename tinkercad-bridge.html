IsokoSense Tinkercad Bridge

Forward Tinkercad serial output → your live dashboard

⚡ How this works
1
Run Tinkercad simulation Start your Arduino UNO circuit in Tinkercad and open the Serial Monitor.
2
Copy Serial output Copy lines from Tinkercad's Serial Monitor that start with PAYLOAD:.
3
Paste below & send Paste them into the box here. The bridge parses the JSON and POSTs to your API.
4
See it on the dashboard Your AquaSure dashboard at http://localhost:5173 will update in real-time.
